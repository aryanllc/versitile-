The complete toolbox

Big ideas, less friction.

One clean starting point for practical office tasks, deep statistical analyses and creative media experiments without switching apps.

Words & Notepad

Rich document creation with typography, tables, markdown/PDF export, plus a distraction-free scratchpad with line numbers.

Sheet & Excel

Full spreadsheet grid with formula engine (=SUM, =AVERAGE, =COUNT), number formatting, and instant CSV import & export.

Slide Presentations

Design slide decks with themes, speaker notes, and launch fullscreen presentations directly in your browser.

Charts & Statistics

Vertical & horizontal bar charts, line, donut and radar graphs. Upload data or calculate mean, median, standard deviation and IQR.

Rich Photo Studio

Deep image tuning, aesthetic filters, canvas drawing annotations, text stamping, crop tools and before/after comparison.

Video Editor

Frame stepping, trim range, real-time video color grading, text watermarks, snapshot frame capture and WebM rendering.

Storage Vault & Remasterer

Upload & store files securely on your device disk, monitor real-time browser storage quota, and remaster images using mathematical vision enhancement.

File compressor

Reduce image sizes and gzip text files locally, with controls to balance size and visual fidelity.

Explore 3D

A canvas-based 3D playground for rotating geometry primitives and recording short animation loops.

Play for a minute

Take a quick break with casual offline mini-games including Snake, memory match and Tic-Tac-Toe.

Made to be opened

Useful tools should feel accessible.

Versitile is an open-source front-end suite by Orgination Corporation with a simple idea: let people explore helpful creative and office utilities without needing to create an account first. Keep the interface understandable, keep supported work on-device, and leave room for the community to improve it.

Open-source note: this suite is provided as a standalone single HTML file without restrictive licensing.

Project principles Local-first suite
Simple tools, clearly labelled
Responsive layout for phones and laptops
No account or server upload required by this demo
Honest about browser and device limitations
Room for contributors, testing and future features
